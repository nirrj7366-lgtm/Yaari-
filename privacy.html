{{BUSINESS_NAME}} ("Yaari", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, mobile number, password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Verification details: date of birth, gender, city, government ID type and a photo of your ID, and for caretakers a police verification certificate.
  • Safety details: emergency contact name and mobile, and your location only when you press SOS.
  • Bookings and wallet: bookings, amounts, earnings, withdrawals and your UPI ID for payouts.
  • Technical data: basic device and usage information needed to run and secure the service.

We do not store your card or UPI payment credentials. Payments are handled by Razorpay.

2. Why we use it

  • To verify that members are adults and genuine.
  • To run bookings, payments, refunds and payouts.
  • To keep members safe, handle SOS alerts and investigate reports.
  • To contact you about your account and provide support.
  • To follow the law and prevent fraud.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other members see only limited profile details: first name, age, city, bio, services and rate. They never see your ID, mobile number or email.
  • Our verification and support team can see your details and ID, and each time an admin opens an ID document it is logged.
  • Service providers that help us operate: Supabase (database and file storage), Razorpay (payments) and Netlify (website hosting).
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed afterwards for safety, fraud prevention, accounting and legal requirements. You can ask us to delete your account and data, and we will do so unless the law requires us to keep something.

5. Security

ID documents are kept in private storage that only you and our authorised admins can open. Access is protected by login and database rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing {{EMAIL}}. We reply within a reasonable time as required by applicable Indian data-protection law.

7. Children

Yaari is only for people aged 18 and above. If we learn that a minor has registered, we will remove the account.
