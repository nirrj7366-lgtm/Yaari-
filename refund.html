Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: {{DATE}}

This policy explains cancellations and refunds for bookings made on Yaari, operated by {{BUSINESS_NAME}}.

1. How payment works

You pay when you send a booking request. The companion then accepts or declines. Their earnings are released only after the meeting is completed and you confirm it.

2. When you get a full refund

  • The companion declines your request, or does not accept it.
  • You cancel before the meeting starts (while the booking is "Waiting for companion" or "Confirmed").
  • The companion cancels or does not show up.
  • We cancel the booking for safety or rule reasons.

A full refund includes the safety and service fee.

3. After the meeting has started

Once the check-in code has been entered and the meeting is in progress, the booking is normally not refundable. If something went wrong, report it within 48 hours at {{EMAIL}}. We review each case and may give a full or partial refund, particularly for safety issues or rule violations.

4. Unpaid or failed payments

If money is deducted but the booking does not show as paid, it is usually confirmed automatically within a few minutes. If it is not, write to {{EMAIL}} with your booking details and we will fix it or refund you.

5. How and when you receive the money

Refunds go back to the original payment method. They are started right away from our side. Banks usually take about 5 to 7 working days to show the money in your account.

6. Companion earnings and withdrawals

Earnings are added to your wallet when the renter confirms the meeting. Withdrawals are paid to your UPI ID after a check, usually within 1 to 2 working days. If a booking is refunded after a payout, we may adjust it against your future earnings.

7. Contact

Questions about a refund: {{EMAIL}} or {{PHONE}}.
